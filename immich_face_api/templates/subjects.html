Help the face API tell who a photo is about. For every face in a photo choose:

  • Main: the photo is about this person (portrait, the speaker at the podium, the person receiving an award). At most one per photo.
  • Co-subject: one of two or a few people the photo is about (a handshake, two people talking, a small group posing).
  • Background: everyone else (the audience, people behind, small or blurred faces, one of many in a chamber or crowd shot). This is the default.

Click a box to cycle its role, or use the buttons under the photo. If a photo is unclear, choose "Not sure". Only the face boxes, the measured signals and your labels are kept, never the photo.
